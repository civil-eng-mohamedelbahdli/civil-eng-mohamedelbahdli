Who I Am

About Me

I'm a Civil Engineer specialising in structural analysis and site execution, with hands-on experience supervising residential buildings up to 10 floors and leading field teams of 10+ workers.

My engineering approach is built on three pillars: accuracy in structural calculations, precision in drawing review, and discipline on the construction site — ensuring every project meets both design intent and safety standards.

I also hold foundational knowledge in web security & cybersecurity, giving me a unique cross-domain perspective in the digital transformation of construction.
